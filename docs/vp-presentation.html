Build and runShip one straightforward app and one with a real integration. Add production access, deployment, monitoring, and a support owner.
DecideCompare delivery time, support load, security findings, and three-year cost, including engineers' time away from product work.

Move apps gradually if the team can operate them safely and the numbers work. Otherwise, keep Power Apps for routine tools and use Devin for custom work.
